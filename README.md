PyRobbo
=======

This is a clone of an old 8-bit Atari game Robbo, written in PyGame.

A little robot needs to collect all parts of his capsule and escape
hostile planets.

The game has been originally written for Atari XE/XL by Janusz Pelc
from "LK. Avalon".

Graphics and level data are taken from the gnurobbo project
(http://gnurobbo.sourceforge.net/).

## Choosing a level

PyRobbo opens with your last played level selected. Use Up/Down to choose
**Level set** or **Level**, then Left/Right to change it. The preview shows
the selected board. Press Enter or click **Start level** to play.

You can also click the selector arrows, or use a joystick's stick or D-pad
to navigate and its first button to start. During play, Esc or Ctrl+Shift+L
returns to the menu. Ctrl+L still jumps directly to a two-digit level number.
